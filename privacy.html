Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version

The app is free. There are no ads, and we never sell or share your information with advertisers. We keep only what the app needs to work, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What we keep

Your email address and password (to sign in) and the name you choose. The groups you create or join, their reading plans, devotionals, prayer requests and memory verses. Which chapters and devotionals you read, on which day, and your active reading time (or, for a printed Bible, the rough time you choose to add). How long you spend memorising verses each day. Which class sessions you attended and your answers to their questions. Your quiz answers. Your notes and reflections, so they’re there on any phone you sign in on. Questions, replies and voice notes you post in a group. A notification address for your phone, if you allow notifications.

What stays on your phone

Highlights, your own memory verses, your verse of the day, the verses you save in Scroll, how you said you feel today and your reading settings are kept on your phone only. Reading as a guest also stays on your phone, and comes with you if you then create an account.

Who can see what

Your notes and reflections are private: only you can see them. Only members of a group see that group, its plan, its questions, prayer requests and voice notes. Members see whether you have read today’s passage and how many quiz questions you’ve answered. Only a group’s admins see reading and memorising time, quiz results, class attendance and the weekly dashboard. Nobody outside your groups can see your name or activity. How you say you feel in Scroll, and the verses you save there, stay on your phone and are never sent to us.

Reporting and blocking

If someone’s post in a group feels wrong, tap Report under it. The group’s admins see that it was reported, not who reported it, and can remove it. You can also block someone: you stop seeing their questions, replies and prayer requests, and they aren’t told. We keep the report until the post is removed or an admin clears it, and your block list until you unblock someone or delete your account.

Who stores it

Your information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and app updates and notifications are delivered through Expo. They process it only to run the app for us.

Children

The app is for people 13 and older. Children under 13 need a parent or guardian to set it up with them.

Deleting your account

Go to You → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, your reading history, notes, quiz answers, class attendance, and the questions, replies and voice notes you posted. Groups you created are handed to another member, or deleted if you were the only one.

Contact us

Questions, a problem, or a request about your information? Email support@scrollcheckpoint.com, or tap Settings → Help and feedback in the app. We reply as soon as we can.
