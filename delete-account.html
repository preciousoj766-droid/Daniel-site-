Delete your account

You can do it yourself in the app, any time

In the app

  1. Open Daniel and go to the You tab
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete my account
  3. Confirm. It happens straight away

Without the app

Email support@scrollcheckpoint.com from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We’ll do it within 7 days and confirm by email

What gets deleted

This permanently removes your account, your reading history, notes, quiz answers, class attendance, and the questions, replies and voice notes you posted. Groups you created are handed to another member, or deleted if you were the only one.

What we keep

Nothing tied to you. Database backups are overwritten within 7 days
