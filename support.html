How can we help?

We read every message

Email us

support@scrollcheckpoint.com

Tell us what phone you use and what happened, and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can. In the app you can also go to Settings → Help and feedback

Something wrong in a group

Tap Report under the post. The group’s admins see it was reported, not who reported it, and can remove it. To stop seeing someone's posts, tap Block. You can unblock people in Settings → Blocked people

Forgot your password

Email us from the address you signed up with and we’ll help you get back in

Need someone to talk to

Daniel isn’t a crisis service. If you’re struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or find free, confidential help near you at findahelpline.com. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number
